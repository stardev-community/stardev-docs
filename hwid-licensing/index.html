HWID licensing, without the fantasy

A HWID check stops casual sharing. It does not stop a determined attacker, and it should never be treated as proof of identity.

What it actually buys you

  • One key, N devices — the most common commercial rule.
  • Visible history: which devices used a key and when.
  • A reason to revoke a key that is being passed around.

What it does not buy: protection against someone patching your client, or against a leaked key used on one device at a time.

How many devices

ProductReasonable default

Reseller stock1 slot until first activation

Reset policy

Self-service reset once per 30 days, second reset on request, third and beyond manual. Log every reset with the old and new identifier — that log is what lets you spot a key being resold.

Privacy

Store a salted hash, not the raw identifier, and delete device history when a key is deleted. You need to recognise a returning device, not to know who owns it.
